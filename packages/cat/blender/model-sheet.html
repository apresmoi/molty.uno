Cat model sheet

Phase 1 of the cat rebuild: the model and rig only, for sign-off before any animation or device code.

Each pose twice: left, the Blender model in flat palette colours; right, the same pose as Molty would draw it (52 x 32 cells of 2 x 4 dots, one outline, brighter on the near side).

The body and hind legs are one merged shape: a long, level body; the thigh forms the rear end with the tail root on top; a narrow shin, a high hock over a near-vertical metatarsus. The white chest is surface colour only. Sitting, the back rounds and the whole hind foot lies flat.

Walk keys come from a gait model: a lateral sequence walk (left hind, left fore, right hind, right fore, a quarter cycle apart), as in Muybridge's cat plates. Keys are timed on the near hind paw.

Rig: root on the floor; pelvis, spine, chest, neck, head; two ears; 12 tail bones; front legs scapula, upper arm, forearm, paw; hind legs thigh, shin, hock (metatarsus), paw. IK targets on all four paws, the hock rides the hind paw target, elbow and knee poles.

Reference check: the hindquarters

Orange: a real cat walking (Eadweard Muybridge, Animal Locomotion, Plate 716, 1887, public domain), scaled to the height of our tail root and pinned there. Purple: ours. From the tail root, down the back of the thigh to the hock is one convex curve on both: the thigh is the rear end. The body now follows a real cat (long, level, the legs leaving a third of the height under the belly), so the whole trunk lines up; only the big cartoon head stays. Made by overlay_ref.py.
