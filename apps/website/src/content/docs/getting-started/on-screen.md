Everything on the screen comes out of the water and goes back into it. Nothing pops in.

## On the screen

| You see | When | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **molty.uno** | power on | the word condenses out of the fluid, then your creature |
| **Your creature** | most of the time | the duck, the cat or the octopus, with its own moods and small idle moves |
| **Words** | your agent speaks or shows text | one word at a time, in step with the voice |
| **The recording ring** | Molty listens | a ring at the rim until you stop talking |
| **A question** | your agent asks, an agent pairs, a package or an update wants a yes | the question on top, the answers to tap below |
| **A growing ring** | a package installs, or your agent works on something long | a line that fills clockwise from 12 o'clock, then flashes and melts |
| **A scene** | your agent plays a package's drawing | sharp lines on fine dust, for example the zodiac chart |

## The cat

The cat (2.0.0) is modelled and animated in Blender. It sits, stands, lies in a loaf, crouches and
walks, with its own authored move between every pair of those resting poses and into and out of
the walk.

- **Tap the screen** and it walks to the front and sits up, looking at you.
- **Shake Molty** and it is jostled, shakes it off like a wet cat, then comes to the front.
- It has moves of its own: `eat` (a bowl of milk), `yarn` (a ball of yarn) and `walk`.

Over the cat the recording ring is at its best: a wave runs out from the middle, its crest turns
red near the rim and lands as one thin red line. While you speak, small ripples run in from the
rim. When you stop, a wave runs back in and fades.

## Questions

| Answers | Layout |
|---|---|
| 1 to 3 (yes / no, pairing's accept / reject) | arcs of the rim under the question, the first on the left |
| 4 or 5 | rows, each underlined in its colour |

A question forms where a wave from the middle passes, and the scene behind it dims but keeps
moving. Tap an answer and everything breaks into dust that the water carries off. A tap on the
question itself or on the middle answers nothing.

## Scenes

A package teaches Molty new drawings. The first is **astrology**: your agent installs it once (you
tap `install`, a ring shows the download) and then draws the real sky.

- Left alone, a scene stands in high definition: fine dust gathers into sharp lines.
- Touch it and a real wave runs through the drawing.
- On the chart, tap a planet, a sign or a line between two planets: it grows, stays sharp, and
  its reading shows in the middle of the wheel, one word at a time.

More: [Packages and scenes](/moltys/packages/).

## Your hands

| You | Molty (out of the box) |
|---|---|
| tap | ripples the water; answers a question; ends a recording and sends it |
| double tap | Molty listens |
| shake | Molty listens (shake to talk) |
| hold BOOT | Molty listens while you hold, sends when you let go |
| click PWR | screen off and on; a double click is do not disturb; held 1.5 s it says goodbye and powers off |

Every one of these can be changed in the console. A press with nothing said is dropped: your agent
never hears silence.

## Which screens

All of this needs a touch screen; listening and speaking also need a mic and a speaker (the 1.28
has neither). It is sharpest on the 466×466 AMOLED of the Waveshare 1.75C. The screen draws at up to 33 frames a second; 30 is the floor we
hold every effect to. Boards: [Build and flash](/getting-started/hardware/).
