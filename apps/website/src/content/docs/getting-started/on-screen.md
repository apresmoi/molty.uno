---
title: What Molty shows
description: What you see on the round screen, and what your hands do to it.
---

Everything on the screen comes out of the water and goes back into it. Nothing pops in.

## Your creature

Most of the time you see your creature: the duck, the cat or the octopus. It breathes, blinks
and looks around by itself.

The cat walks. Tap the screen and it comes to the front and sits up, looking at you.

## The recording ring

When Molty listens, a ring appears at the rim and stays until you stop talking.

With the cat on screen, a wave runs out from the middle and lands as one thin red line.
While you speak, small ripples run in from the rim.

## Questions

When your agent asks you something, the answers appear for you to tap. Up to three answers
sit on the rim. Four or five become rows.

Tap an answer and the question breaks into dust that the water carries off.

## Scenes

A scene is a drawing your agent puts on the screen, such as the astrology chart. Left alone,
it stands sharp on fine dust. Touch it and a wave runs through it.

On the chart, tap a planet, a sign or a line between two planets. It grows, and a short
reading shows in the middle, one word at a time.

## Install progress

When a package installs, a thin line grows around the rim as the download arrives. It
flashes when the package is in. The same line fills while your agent works on a long job.

## Your hands

| You | Molty |
|---|---|
| tap | ripples the water, answers a question, or ends a recording |
| double tap | listens |
| shake | listens |
| hold BOOT | listens while you hold |
| press PWR | turns the screen off and on |

You can change what each one does on the console's **molty** page.
